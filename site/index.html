Rail use differs across stations and days

Station recovery varies by place; rail use varies across the week. Weekday rhythms differ between stations, while weekend use rose relative to weekdays.

Back on track —
but not back to the same pattern.

Perth has returned to its pre-pandemic level, but Sydney and Melbourne account for nearly all the five-city shortfall. In Victoria, metropolitan journeys fell 22.9% while V/Line journeys grew 19.1%. Weekend-to-weekday ratios increased at 176 of 191 eligible stations. Rail is returning, but not to the same pattern.

Sources & Methods

Data sources

Bureau of Infrastructure and Transport Research Economics: Trainline urban and non-urban network totals, and separate Yearbook city estimates.

Department of Transport and Planning: station entries and monthly train patronage. Station estimates are rounded to 50.

Australian Bureau of Statistics: boundaries and city locations.

Current Trainline Tables 15 and 23 were checked against the official report.

Comparisons

Financial years run July–June. Station recovery compares 2018–2019 with 2024–2025 using the same 220 stations.

Area recovery = total current entries ÷ total baseline entries × 100. Areas describe station locations, not residents’ travel. Equal-area grids retain all 222 current stations.

The historical Trainline workbooks were not obtained for this project; their series remain unreconciled across releases. Recovery charts use separate Yearbook city estimates from one release.

Methods and sensitivity checks

National comparisons

Yearbook city estimates include Sydney intercity, a Melbourne regional-commuter allowance and Brisbane Airtrain. Their service scope differs from Trainline. The waterfall adds changes within the Yearbook series; it does not explain their causes.

Reported non-urban services

Trainline 13 Table 23 covers five listed services totalling 57.87 million journeys in 2024–2025. These are service categories, not passengers’ travel purposes. The table is separate from Trainline’s urban total.

Victorian train journeys

Waffle squares round combined metropolitan and V/Line journeys to whole percentage points. Coach, bus and tram categories are excluded. The slope chart compares two endpoints, without showing intervening years.

Metropolitan totals are 243.19 million then 187.40 million journeys; V/Line totals are 21.04 million then 25.06 million. Metropolitan estimation began using available barrier counts in January 2021. V/Line figures include replacement services.

Data vintage: The full Department of Transport and Planning file extends to June 2026. It records 202.31 million metropolitan and 27.10 million V/Line journeys in 2025–2026. Free-travel periods from November 2025 required alternative estimation. The main comparison ends in 2024–2025.

Station maps

Recovery uses summed entries from the same matched stations within each 2025 local government area. Areas without eligible stations are grey. Both maps use a Melbourne-centred azimuthal equal-area display projection.

The bin map groups all 222 current stations into complete squares in Australian equal-area coordinate system (reference number 3577). Each station contributes to one cell. Cells are not clipped at the coast. A fixed square-root colour scale keeps grid sizes comparable.

Station comparisons

Station entries exclude transfers within a station. Southern Cross excludes gates to regional platforms 1–8. Entry changes do not directly measure train patronage. Stable station identifiers define the matched cohort.

The heatmap selects the twelve largest annual-entry stations from 66 eligible stations. It excludes bands at the 50-entry reporting floor, normal weekdays below 1,000 entries, and band-total discrepancies above 150.

Weekend distribution

Each ratio divides average Saturday and Sunday daily entries by average weekday entries. Each station has equal weight. Whiskers reach observations within 1.5 box lengths. Both years require at least 500 weekday entries; weekend values must exceed the 50-entry floor.

Station recovery sensitivity

Of 220 matched identifiers, 219 pass the default 5,000-entry baseline screen. Each station counts once; histogram intervals span 20 index points.

All eligible extremes remain visible. An index of 100 means the baseline was regained, not 100% growth.
